# B149–B154 — non-custodial preparation, not live certification

The client retains assets in their external institution account. JENIOS provides technology and does not receive deposits, hold investment funds or expose withdrawal functions in this block. Broker approval, user consent, account eligibility and applicable regulatory obligations are separate requirements; a public API alone does not prove them.

B151 compared the exact original engine with a conservative guard overlay using chronological development/evaluation partitions, two assets, three original modes and three fixed round-trip cost scenarios. The intent stream was hypothetical alternating buy/sell, not customer history; calibration was not fabricated. The method produced 36 cells and 1,420 recorded hypothetical trades across repeated variants and scenarios, not independent market trades. Four of 18 evaluation cells were net positive for each variant. No economic superiority was certified and the candidate was not promoted. Historical prices and original code do not constitute a prospective pilot. Spot short-return examples are analytical only, not a claim that spot short orders can be opened.

B152 uses decimal strings and fixed-point integer risk accounting, correct inverse settlement, shared multi-mode reservations, atomic SQLite transactions, idempotence and restart reconciliation. UNKNOWN status blocks new entries rather than resubmitting. Partial cancellation retains risk until explicit closure. All events are simulated. The cumulative loss budget deliberately has no automatic reset: a trading-day rollover protocol is still required for a persistent pilot. Conservative stop calculations cannot guarantee fill prices or a maximum gap loss. This SQLite store is an isolated rehearsal, not the production customer/broker ledger.

B153 contains format-tested cTrader and Kraken request drafts, without sockets, authentication, signing secrets or order transport. cTrader application registration and broker support must be confirmed. Kraken demo availability and product eligibility must be rechecked with the provider; documentation does not mean a connected account. The Kraken draft does not send protective orders and explicitly blocks live readiness. IBKR is documentation-only; no B3 partner has been selected. No OAuth consent or real-account connection was performed.

B154 assesses separate evidence requirements and never enables live execution, even when all evidence labels are supplied. Hashes are not an authenticated issuer or a trading authorization. All live-account paths fail closed.

## Before an external demo / paper account

Select a broker and product available to the intended user; obtain provider approval and permitted data use; establish secure authentication and verified account ownership; homologate contract metadata, costs, protective orders, fills/cancellations and reconnect recovery; deploy an authorized isolated private worker with durable reconciliation; link identity/diagnostic correctly. Only then begin original-core prospective paper evaluation. Real money additionally requires separate explicit authorization of capital and limits and an independent review of the evidence.
