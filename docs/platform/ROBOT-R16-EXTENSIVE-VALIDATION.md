# ROBOT-R16 — Extensive validation block

R16 is a deterministic, offline validation block layered on the already-certified robot safety contracts. It does not alter the R15 prospective cohort and does not use real orders, external Paper, deployment, paid services or commercial performance claims.

Coverage:
- Manual delivery/abstention timing matrix.
- Hybrid volatility, spread, direction, liquidity, pause, open-position and stale-data matrix.
- Reverse market type, side, megatrend exception, pause, existing position, borrow, consent, funding, metadata freshness and spread matrix.
- Manual loss-streak education/pause matrix.
- 500 paired synthetic counterfactual rows, explicitly non-commercial.
- Experiment-infrastructure rehearsal for preregistration lead gates and mutation rejection.

All results are lab validation. They can expose logic defects and prove fail-closed behavior; they do not prove real-market profitability.
