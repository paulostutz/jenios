# ROBOT-R25 — Exchange Cost & Outage Stress

R25 adds configurable cost-profile scenarios and operational-failure stress for UltraFast:
- maker/taker friction;
- spread, slippage and optional session funding;
- non-fill penalty;
- exchange degraded/offline/maintenance states;
- symbol offline;
- stale market data;
- slow order acknowledgements;
- rejection clusters;
- per-asset 10k-attempt stress with outage interruptions.

Profiles are synthetic scenarios, not claimed live fees for any exchange.
No real or external Paper orders are sent.
