# ROBOT-R26 — Fee Evidence & Recovery

R26 adds two protections:
1. fee-evidence provenance: official schedules are references, not proof of the customer's actual tier;
2. post-outage recovery: new entries remain blocked until reconnect, market-data resync, account reconciliation and risk reconciliation are complete.

Official fee references can seed scenarios, but live customer costs must come from a verified account/tier context when available.
No real or external Paper order transport is enabled.
